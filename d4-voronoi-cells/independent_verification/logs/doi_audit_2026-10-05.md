# DOI audit of the bibliography of paper/D4.tex (5 October 2026)

Every DOI in the bibliography was resolved again on 5 October 2026 against
its registry, and the registered title, authors, journal or series, volume,
issue, pages and year were compared with the entry. Crossref was read through
api.crossref.org, DataCite (arXiv, Zenodo, 4TU.ResearchData, TU Delft)
through api.datacite.org, and, where api.crossref.org refused repeated
requests with HTTP 429, the Crossref-registered record was read through
api.openalex.org.

Result: 61 entries carry a DOI and all 61 agree with their registry records.
Seven entries carry no DOI because none is registered for them (Cox73, DB16,
GVL13, Gru87, Rog64, Slo, Thu10).

Changes since the audit of 2 October 2026:

- added CL77 (Choi and Lam, Math. Ann. 231 (1977), 1-18), Hil88 (Hilbert,
  Math. Ann. 32 (1888), 342-350) and Ser77 (Serre, GTM 42), and the Sloane
  tables (no DOI);
- D4Zenodo now cites the concept DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22766562, which DataCite
  records with IsVersionOf links from every version (v1.12.0 is
  10.5281/zenodo.23076993); it resolves to the latest release.

Notes on individual entries:

- BV08, CK07, HM10, MO18, Li25: the registry year is the online date, one
  year before the volume year the entry gives (JAMS 21 (2008), JAMS 20
  (2007), JAMS 23 (2010), Experiment. Math. 27 (2018), Adv. Math. 460 (2025));
  volume and pages agree.
- Vor08: Crossref records the first page 97 and a last page 102 for the item
  that carries this DOI; the title, journal, volume, year and first page agree,
  and the memoir runs to page 178 as cited.
- CKM17, Via17, CZ14, Sch42: Crossref gives no page range (an article id, or
  nothing); the cited ranges are those printed by the journals.
- Vir20: Crossref lists 122 authors, the named authors of the article and the
  SciPy 1.0 contributors; the entry gives the named authors and "SciPy 1.0
  Contributors" as the article does.
- GC26: registered as published online 21 May 2026 in Math. Program. Comput.,
  with no volume yet.
