# DOI audit of the bibliography of paper/D4.tex (2 October 2026)

Every DOI in the bibliography was resolved online against its registry record.
Crossref was queried through api.crossref.org.
DataCite was queried through api.datacite.org; it holds the arXiv, Zenodo, 4TU.ResearchData and TU Delft DOIs.
Where Crossref rate-limited the requests, the record was read from OpenAlex (api.openalex.org) instead.
OpenAlex mirrors the Crossref metadata, and it also holds the Preprints.org DOI, which Crossref registers as posted content.
For each DOI, the registered title was compared with the title of its \bibitem; so were the authors, volume, year and first page.

66 entries, 59 DOIs, 7 entries without a DOI (books and reports that have none: Cox73, DB16, GVL13, Gru87, Rog64, Slo, Thu10).
**Result: all 59 DOIs resolve to the work cited.**

Points worth recording:

- FT42: Crossref registers the title as *Über die dichteste Kugelpackung*. The bibliography uses *Über die dichteste Kugellagerung*, the title as printed in Math. Z. 48 and as the literature cites it. The DOI, volume and pages agree.
- BBB26: the version suffix `.v11` resolves (Preprints.org, 26 August 2026). The 25 September audit could not confirm it.
- BV08, CK07, HM10, dLV15, MOA11: the registry gives the online-first year, one year before the volume year printed in the bibliography; the volumes and pages agree.
- D4Zenodo: the record is version v1.12.0, with the title *Supplementary code for Voronoi Cells of Four-Dimensional Unit-Ball Packings*, as cited.
- Zie95: OpenAlex conflates this book with Ziegler's *Lectures on 0/1-Polytopes*; Crossref has *Lectures on Polytopes*, as cited.
